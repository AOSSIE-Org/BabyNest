# Privacy Policy for BabyNest

**Last Updated:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction

BabyNest ("BabyNest," "we," "our," or "us") is a pregnancy-tracking and
wellness application designed to help users organize pregnancy-related
information, appointments, reminders, and related records.

This Privacy Policy explains what information BabyNest may handle, how
it is used and stored, when it may be shared, and the choices available
to you.

BabyNest is intended to support personal organization and general
pregnancy-related information. It is not a substitute for advice,
diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

## 2. Information We Handle

The information handled depends on the features you choose to use and
the version of the app.

### A. Information You Enter

You may choose to enter information such as:

-   Profile details, such as your name or profile image.
-   Pregnancy information, such as the last menstrual period (LMP),
    estimated due date, pregnancy week, or cycle length.
-   Health and wellness records, such as weight, blood pressure,
    symptoms, medicines, and discharge logs.
-   Appointments, reminders, tasks, notes, and related dates or times.
-   Messages or prompts you submit to BabyNest's AI assistant.

Some of this information may be sensitive personal and health-related
information. Please enter only information you want to store in the app.

### B. Information Stored on Your Device

BabyNest uses local app storage, including a SQLite database and app
preferences, to support features such as profile information, pregnancy
records, appointments, tasks, and wellness logs. Depending on the
feature, some preferences or profile information may be stored using the
device's application storage.

Information stored locally remains on your device unless you delete it,
remove the app, or a feature transmits it elsewhere. Uninstalling the
app may remove locally stored information, but you should not rely on
uninstalling as a guaranteed deletion method for every type of data or
backup.

### C. AI Assistant and On-Device Processing

BabyNest may use a language model to interpret prompts, extract
information, or generate responses.

When the app uses a model that runs entirely on your device, the prompt
and model processing for that feature take place locally and are not
sent to a model provider by that local inference process.

This statement applies only to on-device model processing. If a version
of BabyNest includes or enables a remote AI service, server-backed
assistant, or other online feature, the information submitted to that
feature may be transmitted to the relevant server or service provider.
Any such feature must be described accurately in the app's disclosures
and in this policy before release. But for now, the AI model runs entirely on-device and that prompts are not sent to a remote service.

AI-generated responses can be incomplete or incorrect. Do not rely on
the AI assistant for emergency guidance, medical diagnosis, or treatment
decisions.

### D. Technical Information

The app or its underlying platform and libraries may process limited
technical information needed to operate the app, such as
operating-system information, app version, error details, or diagnostic
information. Whether diagnostic information is transmitted off the
device depends on the SDKs and services included in the published build.

This policy should be updated if analytics, crash-reporting,
advertising, or other third-party SDKs are added.

## 3. How We Use Information

BabyNest uses information, as applicable, to:

-   Display and maintain your profile and pregnancy-related records.
-   Calculate or display pregnancy dates and related tracking
    information.
-   Manage appointments, tasks, and reminders.
-   Display weight, blood pressure, symptoms, medicine, and other logs
    you choose to maintain.
-   Process prompts and provide AI-assisted features.
-   Save app preferences and maintain app functionality.
-   Diagnose errors, maintain security, and improve reliability where
    relevant.
-   Respond to support requests and comply with applicable legal
    obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use health information
for advertising.

## 4. Storage, Processing, and Sharing

### A. Local Storage

Records saved to BabyNest's local database or app preferences are stored
within the app's private storage area on your device. Access to this
storage is subject to the device's security and any access granted to
people or software that can unlock or otherwise access your device.

### B. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on operating-system functionality or third-party
libraries to provide features such as local notifications or model
inference. A library or service may process information according to its
role and configuration.

BabyNest does not intentionally share personal information with third
parties for their own advertising or sale. If an online backend, cloud
storage provider, remote AI provider, analytics provider, or
crash-reporting service is included in a release, its role and the
information it receives must be reflected in this policy and in Google
Play's Data safety disclosures.

We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably
necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the
service, subject to applicable law.

## 5. Notifications and Permissions

If you enable reminders or notifications, BabyNest may use your device's
notification functionality to show reminders you have configured, such
as appointments or tasks. Notification permissions are controlled
through Android settings and can be changed there.

The app should request sensitive device permissions only when needed for
a feature and should explain their purpose before requesting them.

## 6. Data Security

BabyNest uses app-private local storage and reasonable measures intended
to reduce unauthorized access. However, no method of electronic storage
or transmission is completely secure. The security of information on
your device also depends on your device settings, operating-system
security, and who can access the device.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records are generally retained until you delete them,
clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to how your device
and operating system handle app storage and backups.

You can delete or clear records using available in-app controls, where
provided. If you need help removing information or if a published
version of BabyNest stores information on a server, contact us using the
contact details published with the app. For server-held information, we
will explain the applicable deletion process and handle verified
requests in accordance with applicable law and legitimate retention
requirements.

**Before publishing:** ensure that all in-app delete/reset functions
remove the relevant records and that any account-based or server-side
data has a working deletion process. If users can create an account,
provide an in-app account-deletion path and an external account-deletion
request URL as required by Google Play.

## 8. Children's Privacy

BabyNest is intended for people who meet the minimum age requirements
applicable in their location. It is not designed to knowingly collect
personal information from children who are below the applicable minimum
age. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us,
contact us so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate steps
taken.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to
access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal information.

You can choose which information to enter into BabyNest, manage
notification permissions through your device settings, and use available
in-app controls to edit or delete records. You may also contact us with
privacy questions or requests.

## 10. International Users and Legal Compliance

Your privacy rights may vary depending on where you live. We will handle
privacy requests in accordance with applicable law. If BabyNest begins
transmitting information to servers or service providers in other
countries, this policy should be updated to explain the relevant
processing and transfers.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, data
practices, or legal requirements change. We will revise the "Last
Updated" date when we publish a new version. Please review this policy
periodically.
