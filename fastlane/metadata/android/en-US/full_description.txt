BabyNest is a pregnancy tracking application designed to help
expectant parents organize pregnancy-related information and
daily activities.

Features:
- Task Management: View tasks & appointments by pregnancy week with priority sorting
- Health Tracking: Weight, symptoms, medications, blood pressure
- Manage pregnancy related tasks and appointments.
- Visualize pregnancy progress with a week-by-week timeline in graphical form.
- Store structured app data in a local SQLite database.
- Access an AI assistant powered by a local language model

BabyNest uses local data storage for supported app information.
Review the app's privacy policy and settings for details about
data storage, model downloads and network access.
